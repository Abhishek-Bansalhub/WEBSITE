THE FIRM

Built for matters where law and strategy meet.

Rai Associates is a litigation-focused legal practice working across a broad range of civil, commercial, financial, regulatory, constitutional and criminal matters.

The practice is designed for disputes that move across forums — from statutory authorities and tribunals to the High Courts — requiring careful pleadings, focused research, effective advocacy and disciplined case strategy.
